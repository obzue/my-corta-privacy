Privacy Policy & Data Protection Notice

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

App Name: My Corta Companion™

Developer: ObzueAI

1. Overview & On-Device Security

My Corta Companion™ is committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. All user files, contact lists, financial records, voice biometric templates, and child/senior location logs are encrypted locally on your device using AES-256 encryption. ObzueAI does not sell or share personal identifiable information (PII) with third parties.

2. Data Access & Sensor Delegation

To operate as your personal voice assistant and device guardian, My Corta Companion™ requires access to your device's Camera, Microphone, Geolocation (GPS), and Local Storage. Microphone and camera access are used exclusively for voice interaction, emergency recording commands ("Corta, record video/audio"), and safety checks.

3. Under-18 Child & Senior Protection

For accounts designated under 18 or in Senior Protection Mode, location tracking operates in an Always-On state. Settings are tamper-proof and locked to the paired master parent/caregiver device. Geofence data and remote audio monitoring are restricted solely to authorized account guardians.

4. Anonymized Voice Sampling for AI Learning

To improve conversational understanding, brief, non-continuous audio clips (1–3 seconds) may be randomly sampled. All samples are automatically stripped of personal identifiable information (PII) and unlinked from your account ID or IP address before model training. Users can opt out anytime in app settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact ObzueAI support at support@obzueai.com.
