Account & Data Deletion Request

App Name: My Corta Companion™

Developer: ObzueAI

Request Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your My Corta Companion account and all associated personal data stored on our servers, please fill out the form below or contact our support team at support@obzueai.com.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

  • Account Access: Your login credentials and user profile will be permanently deactivated.
  • Server Data: All associated cloud storage data, profile information, and sync settings will be deleted within 30 days.
  • Local Device Data: Local files and voice print templates on your phone/PC can be cleared by uninstalling the application.
